Adjust calorie target

Calorie needs vary between people. You can fine-tune the target (1,200–6,000 kcal). Changing it re-suggests your macros.

Macro targets

Method: protein 1.6 g per kg of body weight (1.8 g/kg when losing weight, capped at 35% of calories); fat 25% of calories; carbohydrates fill the rest. Protein and carbs give 4 kcal/g, fat 9 kcal/g.
